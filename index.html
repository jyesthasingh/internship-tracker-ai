Internship Tracker AI

What actually breaks when a student applies to 30 roles, and can a small AI-assisted tool fix it?

In progress: findings being added Built solo, tested with classmates By Jyestha Singh

The question

What do students lose track of when applying to many internships and jobs, and which one or two features would fix it?

How I'm studying it

Four steps. Every number on this page comes from my own interviews and real usage.

STEP 1

Interview classmates

Talk to 5 classmates who are applying to many roles. How do they track applications today, and where does it break?

Not started
STEP 2

Scope a small v1

Pick 3 to 4 features backed by the interviews, and write down what is cut and why.

Not started
STEP 3

Build and test the AI

Build v1, then check the AI suggestion against a written set of test cases and log what fails.

Not started
STEP 4

Ship, log, iterate

Give it to the same classmates, log real usage, cut what is ignored, and write the v2 roadmap.
