# Interview guide (15 minutes), classmates applying to roles

**Opening:** "I'm building a small tool for tracking applications. Can I ask how you do it today?"

1. How many roles have you applied to in the last month?
2. Where do you track them today? Show me if you can.
3. Tell me about a time you missed a deadline or lost track of an application.
4. How do you decide which resume version to send?
5. What do you forget most: follow-ups, deadlines, or which version went where?
6. What have you tried (sheets, Notion, apps) and why did it stop working?
7. If a tool did one thing perfectly, what would it be?
8. Would you use something like this next week? What would stop you?
